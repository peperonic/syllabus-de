## Key question 1: KMK Bildungsstandards for Mathematik, Chemie, Physik (plus EPA)

### Takeaway
All relevant standards are PDFs on kmk.org under /fileadmin/Dateien/veroeffentlichungen_beschluesse/. Mathematik (Primar, ESA/MSA) and Naturwissenschaften (MSA) were revised in 2022 and 2024 respectively ("Weiterentwickelte Bildungsstandards", WeBiS); the Abitur standards are from 2012 (Mathematik) and 2020 (Chemie, Physik) and replaced the EPA from the Abitur 2024/25 onward. kmk.org's Nutzungsbedingungen are restrictive (private non-commercial copies only, written consent for databases), so the standards should be ingested as structure (Kompetenzbereiche, Leitideen, Basiskonzepte, Inhaltsbereiche) rather than full text unless the Land versions (which may be amtliche Werke) are used.

### Inferences
- The 2024 WeBiS Chemie and Physik (MSA) documents are the current national reference for Sek I science topic coverage; the 2004 Vereinbarung remains relevant only for Länder whose Lehrpläne have not yet been adapted (adaptation timing is Land-specific and not stated in the KMK documents).
- The Mathe 2022 and Nawi 2024 standards state no implementation Schuljahr; the only hard dates are the IQB-Bildungstrend checks (2027 Primar, 2030 Sek I), so Land curricula may lag the standards until then.
- Legal status: the Bildungsstandards are Beschlüsse of a Länder coordination body; the Wolters Kluwer copyright line on the AHR Chemie/Physik editions and the KMK Nutzungsbedingungen argue against treating the kmk.org PDFs as freely reusable amtliche Werke (§ 5 UrhG). The versions enacted by Länder as Verwaltungsvorschriften (e.g. in Amtsblätter) are more likely to qualify as amtliche Werke, but this is a legal assessment the user should confirm. For the knowledge base: ingest the structure (Kompetenzbereiche, Leitideen, Basiskonzepte, Inhaltsbereiche, ESA/MSA differences) with short quotations and citations; avoid wholesale full-text ingestion of the kmk.org PDFs.

### Gaps
- EPA Chemie and EPA Physik: the KMK pages fetched do not list them; from prior knowledge both are Beschlüsse of 01.12.1989 i.d.F. 05.02.2004 at https://www.kmk.org/fileadmin/Dateien/veroeffentlichungen_beschluesse/1989/1989_12_01-EPA-Chemie.pdf and .../1989_12_01-EPA-Physik.pdf (unverified). Whether any Land still applies them after the Abitur 2025 was not confirmed; the 2020 standards declare them fully replaced from Abitur 2024/25.
- The 2004 Vereinbarung Bio/Che/Phy PDF was listed on the KMK page but not opened; its inner structure (Kompetenzbereiche Fachwissen, Erkenntnisgewinnung, Kommunikation, Bewertung; Basiskonzepte) was not re-verified in this session.
- The nature of the "18.06.2026" note in the Mathe ESA/MSA PDF could not be determined.

## Key question 2: IQB resources (Abituraufgabenpools, Operatoren, Formelsammlung, Hilfsmittel, VERA, Kompetenzstufenmodelle, Bildungstrend)

### Takeaway
The IQB publishes Abitur pool tasks (Mathematik since 2017, Biologie/Chemie/Physik since 2025) as per-task PDFs in a filterable online browser, plus an extensive set of begleitende Dokumente (Operatoren, Hilfsmittel rules valid through 2029 and from 2030, the Formelsammlung, Kriterien, Inhaltliche Vereinbarungen, Kompetenzerläuterungen). VERA-3/8 cover Mathematik but not the sciences. No licence is stated anywhere on iqb.hu-berlin.de; the pools are published only where third-party rights could be cleared, and the Impressum has no reuse clause, so treat IQB tasks as all rights reserved and use them for cross-checking, not ingestion.

### Inferences
- The IQB Operatorenlisten and the Inhaltliche Vereinbarungen (per subject) are compact national statements of what is examinable in the Abitur and are the most efficient cross-check for Oberstufe topic coverage; the Formelsammlung indicates which formulae pupils are expected to recognise.
- Because the IQB publishes no licence and clears third-party rights per task, the safe assumption for the knowledge base is all rights reserved: store metadata (year, subject, Anforderungsniveau, Sachgebiet, Leitidee) and links, not task text.
- The Bildungstrend 2024 points to Mathematik and Chemie at MSA level as the weakest areas nationally; the report does not (on the summary page) break weakness down by Leitidee, so a learning assistant cannot derive topic-level weak spots from the summary alone; the full Berichtsband and the Ergebnisbrowser would be needed.

## Key question 3: Other national or cross-state framework documents (GDSU, MNU, GDM/GDCP/DPG, Mathematik-Kommission, cosh)

### Takeaway
Non-governmental bodies provide useful cross-state comparisons and transition catalogues: the cosh Mindestanforderungskatalog Mathematik (Version 3.1, updated 2025) for the Schule-Hochschule interface, the Mathematik-Kommission's material "Mathe = Mathe? ## Key question 4: School landscape structure (school forms, G8/G9, Abschlüsse, Stundentafeln, statistics, Eurydice)

## Key question 5: Which sources are amtliche Werke or openly licensed, and what that means for ingestion

### Takeaway
Only Destatis GENESIS data (dl-de/by-2-0) and some IQB and Destatis graphics (CC BY 4.0) are explicitly openly licensed. kmk.org applies restrictive Nutzungsbedingungen, the AHR Chemie/Physik standards carry a Wolters Kluwer copyright, iqb.hu-berlin.de states no licence and clears rights per task, and the association sites (Mathematik-Kommission) state "Alle Rechte vorbehalten". Ingest full text only from the openly licensed sources; from the rest ingest structure, metadata and short cited quotations.

### Inferences
