## Baden-Württemberg (BW): all key questions

### Takeaway
BW is in the middle of a curriculum transition: the Bildungsplan 2016 (V1, point updates V2) is being replaced by "V3.0" Fachpläne for the new G9 Gymnasium and the Gemeinsamer Bildungsplan der Sekundarstufe I, published online in July 2026 and phased in grade by grade from 2025/26 (Klassen 5 and 6) to 2032/33 (Klassen 5 to 13). The portal is a CMS web app with stable human-readable plan identifiers; robots.txt blocks PDFs for all crawlers; the portal carries a "ZSL, Alle Rechte vorbehalten" notice and no open licence.

### Inferences
- The Gemeinsamer Bildungsplan der Sekundarstufe I is the plan for Hauptschule/Werkrealschule, Realschule and Gemeinschaftsschule (Sek I); the portal pages do not state this explicitly, it follows from BW school law and from the Gemeinschaftsschule profile subjects listed in the Sek I index.
- Grade in which Physik and Chemie start: Gymnasium G9 neu Physik Klasse 7, Chemie Klasse 8 (from the Beispielcurricula grade bands KL-07-08 for PH and KL-08-10 for CH, and the Landtag table layout); Sek I Physik and Chemie Klasse 7 (BC KL-07-09). Under Bildungsplan 2016 (V1/V2) both subjects have standards for Klassen 7/8 with BNT in 5/6.
- Oberstufe: V1/V2 Gymnasium plans cover G8 Klassen 5 to 12 with Oberstufe Klassen 11/12 (Leistungsfach and Basisfach since Abitur 2021); V3.0 covers G9 Klassen 5 to 13 with Klasse 11 as Einführungsphase and 12/13 Leistungsfach/Basisfach. The first G9-neu Abitur under V3.0 will be 2034 (Klasse 13 reached 2032/33, per the FAQ table it is 2032/33).
- Legal instrument: BW Bildungspläne are enacted as Verwaltungsvorschrift des Kultusministeriums and published in the Amtsblatt "Kultus und Unterricht" (Lehrplanhefte); the portal's FAQ refers to "Inkraftsetzungsformeln" inside each plan and the KM letter gives entry-into-force dates. This is background knowledge, not verified against a K.u.U. issue in this run.
- § 5 UrhG assessment (assessment only): the Bildungsplan text as a published Verwaltungsvorschrift is a candidate for § 5 Abs. 1 UrhG (amtliche Erlasse und Bekanntmachungen). The portal nevertheless asserts "Alle Rechte vorbehalten" (ZSL), so expect the publisher to treat at least layout, Beispielcurricula and Umsetzungshilfen as protected; those supplementary materials are at most § 5 Abs. 2 (Änderungsverbot and Quellenangabe) and more likely ordinary copyrighted works.
- robots.txt blocking of .pdf for all crawlers plus the Crawl-delay of 2 seconds means a polite scraper should read the HTML plan pages (which are the primary publication) and treat Beispielcurricula PDFs as robots-disallowed; clarify rights with ZSL (poststelle@zsl.kvbwl.de, named in the Impressum) before bulk PDF download.

## Bayern (BY): all key questions

### Takeaway
Bayern publishes all general-education Lehrpläne (LehrplanPLUS) as a single ISB web application at lehrplanplus.bayern.de with a clean URL scheme per Schulart, Jahrgangsstufe and Fach, including Gymnasium Jahrgangsstufe 13 (G9). The Nutzungsbedingungen state explicitly that Lehrplan texts are not protected by copyright and may be used commercially; the Lehrplanverzeichnis Bekanntmachung (KWMBl. 2016 S. 264, amended BayMBl. 2021 Nr. 220) anchors the legal status under Art. 45 Abs. 2 BayEUG. Gesamtlehrplan PDFs are offline until Q4 2026 and a redesign of the portal was announced for 2026/27, so the URL scheme may change.

- Separate subjects: Physik starts Jgst 8 and Chemie Jgst 9 (Jgst 8 at NTG) at the Gymnasium; Natur und Technik (NT) is the integrated science subject Jgst 5 to 7. ## Hessen (HE): all key questions

### Takeaway
Hessen publishes Kerncurricula (KC) as PDFs on the ministry's Drupal site kultus.hessen.de: Primarstufe and Sekundarstufe I KC (binding since 2011/12, one PDF per Bildungsgang Hauptschule, Realschule, Gymnasium for each subject, plus Leitfäden), and the revised Kerncurricula gymnasiale Oberstufe (KCGO) in force since the half-year change of 2024/25 for the Einführungsphase and first basis of the Landesabitur 2027 (Mathematik and Physik "Ausgabe 2024", Chemie "April 2026"). The site asserts copyright and requires approval for republication; no open licence.
