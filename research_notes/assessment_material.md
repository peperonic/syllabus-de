# Assessment-side and "expected knowledge" materials for Mathematik, Chemie, Physik (German school system, status November 2026)

Scope note: this file maps the categories of official or semi-official material that make expected knowledge more concrete than a curriculum (exam focus documents, published exam papers, basic-knowledge catalogues, operator lists and formula sheets, school-to-university transition catalogues, textbook-derived cross-checks, competitions). It does not enumerate curricula per Land. Every URL carries a verification date (the date a fetch resolved and returned content) or "unverified" (URL seen only in search results or in a verified aggregator page, not fetched). All verification dates are 2026-11-07 unless stated. Site fetch blocks encountered: isb.bayern.de HTML pages, bildungsserver.berlin-brandenburg.de, schleswig-holstein.de (robots), lehrplanplus.bayern.de, mathe-wettbewerbe.de HTML pages (PDFs on isb.bayern.de did resolve).

## Key question 1: Abitur focus documents per Land (what is published annually about exam content, format, rights)

### Takeaway
Every Land with a Zentralabitur publishes some annual "focus" document, but the depth varies enormously: NRW (Abiturvorgaben with "Fokussierungen" per Inhaltsfeld, three years ahead), Berlin/Brandenburg (Prüfungsvorgaben per subject and course level, two years ahead), Hessen (Abiturerlass with per-subject "inhaltliche Schwerpunktsetzungen") and Hamburg (A-Heft with Schwerpunktthemen and Operatoren) are the most concrete; Niedersachsen publishes Hinweise only for Mathematik and Physik (none for Chemie); Bremen, Sachsen-Anhalt, Schleswig-Holstein mainly describe exam structure, Hilfsmittel and the use of IQB pool tasks. Rights are almost never stated as an open licence; the typical position is implicit amtliches Werk for the Vorgaben, and explicit "all rights reserved" for exam tasks (Hessen, Hamburg). Rheinland-Pfalz (decentral Abitur) and Saarland have no public focus document that this research could find.

### Inferences
- Where a Land draws on the IQB pool (all Länder for Mathematik since 2017 and for Chemie/Physik since 2025 to varying degrees), the Land's own focus document shrinks to structure, Hilfsmittel and selection rules, and the IQB "Inhaltliche Vereinbarungen" plus the KMK Bildungsstandards become the real content specification. NRW is the main exception with topic-level Fokussierungen.
- Legal status: Vorgaben, Erlasse and Hinweise are administrative publications and in practice fall under § 5 UrhG (amtliches Werk) or are at least freely redistributable in substance, but none of the fetched documents states this explicitly; only Bremen says "Nachdruck ist zulässig". Exam tasks themselves are treated as copyrighted (Hessen, Hamburg explicit).

### Gaps
- Baden-Württemberg: no official "Schwerpunktthemen" page for Mathematik/Physik/Chemie Abitur 2027 surfaced in search; zsl-bw.de and km-bw.de were not fetched. Status unknown (Sternchenthemen exist for Deutsch).
- Sachsen, Thüringen, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Saarland, Rheinland-Pfalz: the annual focus documents (if any) were not located; only exam-paper pages were checked.
- The Hamburg A-Heft and Hessen Abiturerlass subject sections for Mathematik/Chemie/Physik were beyond the fetched excerpt; structure confirmed via table of contents only.

## Key question 2: Published past exam papers (Abitur, IQB pool, central Sek I exams) and rights

### Takeaway
Public, login-free Abitur papers in Mathematik exist for Bayern (ISB PDFs), Mecklenburg-Vorpommern (Musterprüfungen with Musterlösungen), Bremen (edited for copyright), Sachsen-Anhalt (older papers), Schleswig-Holstein (2026 Prüfungsaufgaben), Berlin (Physik 2015-2017 under CC BY via ISQ), Hamburg (via Bildungsserver) and nationally via the IQB pool; NRW, Sachsen and Thüringen restrict access (school login, protected area, registration). Only two sources state an open licence (ISQ Berlin Physik: CC BY; cosh catalogue, see Q5). The IQB publishes the drawn pool tasks with Erwartungshorizonte but states no licence. Central Sek I exam tasks are mostly behind portals (Berlin ISQ-Portal, NRW) or scattered (Bayern ISB Realschule, MV Mittlere Reife, Thüringen).

### Inferences
- For a topic checklist, Erwartungshorizonte (where published: IQB, MV, Berlin ISQ Physik) are more valuable than the tasks, because they list the solution steps and thus the operational knowledge expected. The IQB pool, with Mathematik since 2017 and Chemie/Physik since 2025, is the single most consistent national source, but its unstated licence means "structure only / cross-check" use.
- The Sachsen rationale (third-party material embedded in tasks) is the general reason why many Länder do not publish openly; Bremen's "edited for copyright reasons" shows the workaround.

## Key question 3: Catalogues of required basic knowledge (Grundwissen, niveaubestimmende Aufgaben, Kompetenztests, Mindeststandards, KSM)

## Key question 4: Operatorenlisten and formula sheets

### Takeaway
Since the IQB pool became binding, the national "Grundstock von Operatoren" (Mathematik; Naturwissenschaften) and the "Mathematisch-naturwissenschaftliche Formelsammlung" (shared by Mathematik, Biologie, Chemie, Physik) are the de facto standard for what students may look up and which cognitive operations are expected; Länder such as SH, Bremen, Sachsen-Anhalt and NRW reference it directly. The IQB states only that "Für den Druck der Mathematisch-naturwissenschaftlichen Formelsammlung wurden Nutzungsrechte vergeben"; no open licence. State-specific Merkhilfen are being superseded.

## Key question 5: Transition Schule-Hochschule (cosh, Mathematik-Kommission, MNU/DPG)

### Takeaway
The cosh Mindestanforderungskatalog Mathematik Version 3.0 (1 Nov 2021) is the single best-licensed expected-knowledge document found in this research: CC BY-SA 4.0, six topic areas, 108 example tasks, explicitly describing what first-year WiMINT students should know. The Mathematik-Kommission Übergang Schule-Hochschule (DMV, GDM, MNU) publishes statements and a Maßnahmenkatalog under all rights reserved; no comparable catalogue for Physik or Chemie was found.

## Key question 6: Textbook-derived cross-checks (Stoffverteilungspläne, tables of contents, Lernmittel approval lists)

### Takeaway
Publishers (verified: Klett; seen in search: C.C. Buchner, Cornelsen via product pages) offer free Stoffverteilungspläne that map each textbook chapter to the Land's Kernlehrplan codes with hour allocations, which makes them excellent sequencing cross-checks; they carry a plain publisher copyright ("© Ernst Klett Verlag") and must not be ingested. Official Lernmittel approval lists exist in Bayern and NRW (search hits only), while the cross-state Deutscher Bildungsserver overview page could not be fetched.

### Inferences
- Stoffverteilungspläne are the best available proxy for "what is actually taught in which order" per Land, because publishers align them to the current Kernlehrplan version and schools adopt them as Schulcurriculum templates. - Lernmittel approval lists tell which textbook series are in use per Land and Schulart, thus which Stoffverteilungsplan to consult; they are administrative lists (amtlich) but their content is bibliographic, not topical.

### Gaps
