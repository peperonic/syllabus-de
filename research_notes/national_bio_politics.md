## Key question 1: KMK/BMK documents for Biologie

### Takeaway
Biologie has KMK Bildungsstandards at MSA (2004, weiterentwickelt 13.06.2024) and AHR level (18.06.2020); no ESA standards. The AHR standards replaced the EPA Biologie (05.02.2004) from Abitur 2024/25. Basiskonzepte differ between levels: six at MSA 2024, five at AHR 2020. Licence situation as for Chemie/Physik: kmk.org terms restrictive, AHR PDF carries Wolters Kluwer and KMK copyright lines, so ingest structure only.

## Key question 2: KMK/BMK documents for Politik, Wirtschaft, Arbeitslehre and Verbraucherbildung

### Takeaway
Confirmed: there are no KMK Bildungsstandards for Politik/Sozialkunde, Wirtschaft or Arbeitslehre. The national reference points are the EPA Sozialkunde/Politik (1989 i.d.F. 17.11.2005) and EPA Wirtschaft (1989 i.d.F. 16.11.2006), which remain the current national Abitur frames, plus cross-cutting Empfehlungen: Verbraucherbildung (2013, Neufassung 05.06.2025), Demokratiebildung (2009 i.d.F. 11.10.2018), Berufliche Orientierung (Dec 2017), Menschenrechtsbildung (1980 i.d.F. 2018), Europabildung (1978 i.d.F. 2020), Erinnerungskultur (2014, i.d.F. given as 11.06.2026). KMK has no separate paper on Wirtschaftliche Bildung.

## Key question 4: Non-governmental civic and economic education frameworks and providers

### Takeaway
GPJE (2004) and DeGÖB (2004/2006/2009) are the two professional-society "Bildungsstandards" proposals; both are commercial or not openly available, so only their competence vocabularies are usable. The bpb is the largest provider; licences are per text, most commonly CC BY-NC-ND, with Themenblätter OER versions under CC BY-SA 4.0 and izpb/Schriftenreihe not CC. Bundesbank and Teach Economy materials are free but not openly licensed; the vzbv Materialkompass is a useful quality filter.
