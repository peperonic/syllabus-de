# German school curricula source inventory: NW, NI, HH, HB (status November 2026)

Verification date for all "verified" URLs: 2026-11-07 (WebFetch resolved and returned the expected content). "search-found" means the URL appeared in a web search result but was not fetched. Direct curl through the sandbox proxy was blocked (CONNECT 403), so robots.txt and PDFs were checked with WebFetch only.

Cross-state note on § 5 UrhG (assessment, not legal advice): § 5 Abs. 1 UrhG exempts "Gesetze, Verordnungen, amtliche Erlasse und Bekanntmachungen"; § 5 Abs. 2 exempts "andere amtliche Werke, die im amtlichen Interesse zur allgemeinen Kenntnisnahme veröffentlicht worden sind" (with Änderungsverbot and Quellenangabe, §§ 62, 63). In all four states the curriculum text is formally put into force by a Runderlass / Erlass / Bekanntmachung of the ministry or Behörde and is binding on schools. Where the Erlass itself "setzt fest" or "erlässt" the plan (NW RdErl. "werden hiermit Kernlehrpläne ... festgesetzt"; HB Erlass 3/2026 "werden ... Bildungspläne ... erlassen"), an Abs. 1 classification is arguable; the more cautious and common reading treats the separately published plan text (Heft, PDF) as an "anderes amtliches Werk" under Abs. 2. Either way the text is free of copyright protection as an amtliches Werk, but portal operators (QUA-LiS, hamburg.de, BSB PDFs) still print "Alle Rechte vorbehalten" or "ohne schriftliche Genehmigung untersagt". No state portal carries an explicit open licence (no CC) for curricula. Supplementary material (Handreichungen, Beispielcurricula, exam papers) is less likely to be covered by § 5 and should be treated as copyrighted unless stated otherwise.

## Nordrhein-Westfalen (NW)

### Takeaway
NW curricula ("Kernlehrpläne", Primarstufe: "Lehrpläne") are set by Runderlass of the Ministerium für Schule und Bildung under § 29 SchulG, published in ABl. NRW and collected in BASS; the texts live in the QUA-LiS Lehrplannavigator, which moved from schulentwicklung.nrw.de to lehrplannavigator.nrw.de (Drupal). Big transition: new Kernlehrpläne for the gymnasiale Oberstufe (34 subjects, incl. Mathematik, Chemie, Physik) were published 28.08.2026 and take effect 01.08.2027 with the Einführungsphase; the 2013/14 GOSt Kernlehrpläne expire auslaufend on 31.07.2027.

## Niedersachsen (NI)

### Takeaway
NI "Kerncurricula" are issued by the Niedersächsisches Kultusministerium (Herausgeber on every KC) and published in the NLQ database cuvo.nibis.de ("Curriculare Vorgaben für allgemein bildende Schulen und berufliche Gymnasien"), a PHP application with stable numeric download IDs. Mathematik and the sciences in Sek I are mostly 2013 to 2021 documents (Gymnasium Mathematik 2015, Gymnasium Naturwissenschaften 2015); Sek II Chemie and Physik 2022, Mathematik 2018; Grundschule Mathematik is new (1.8.2025). In Sek I, Chemie and Physik have no separate Kerncurricula: they are Fachteile of the "Kerncurriculum Naturwissenschaften" of each school form.

## Hamburg (HH)

### Takeaway
Hamburg's "Bildungspläne" (per subject "Rahmenplan") 2022 to 2024 are issued by the Behörde für Schule und Berufsbildung (now listed as Behörde für Schule, Familie und Berufsbildung) and published on hamburg.de with PDFs on dokumente.hamburg.de; they entered into force in two steps, 1.8.2023 (Grundschule, Studienstufe, Allgemeiner Teil, Mathematik) and 1.8.2024 (all remaining Rahmenpläne incl. Naturwissenschaften und Technik, Physik, Chemie).
