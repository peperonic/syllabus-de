# German school curricula (Mathematik, Chemie, Physik) in BE, BB, SH, MV: verified source inventory

Research date: 2026-11-07. All "verified" URLs below resolved via WebFetch on 2026-11-07 unless marked "unverified". Berlin and Brandenburg share the Rahmenlehrplan 1-10 and the ländergemeinsame GOST Rahmenlehrpläne; they are treated together where the documents are identical, but each state has its own portal, legal instrument and school structure.

Tool budget: about 50 calls were used. Direct curl to the portals was blocked by the sandbox proxy (CONNECT 403), so robots.txt files were read through WebFetch.

## Key question 1: Which ministry and Landesinstitut publish the curricula, on which portals?

### Takeaway
BE and BB curricula are published on the joint Bildungsserver Berlin-Brandenburg, which since 1 January 2025 is run by the Brandenburg-only LIBRA (Landesinstitut Brandenburg für Schule und Lehrkräftebildung) on behalf of both ministries; Berlin mirrors most PDFs on berlin.de. SH curricula ("Fachanforderungen") are published by IQSH on Fachportal.SH (fachportal.lernnetz.de). MV curricula ("Rahmenpläne") are published on the Bildungsserver MV (bildung-mv.de), operated by the Ministerium für Bildung und Kindertagesförderung with IQ M-V doing the Rahmenplanarbeit.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Berlin has no own curriculum portal beyond berlin.de; the Bildungsserver (LIBRA) remains the primary host for the RLP 1-10 amtliche Fassung for both states. A scraper can treat the Bildungsserver as the single source for RLP 1-10 and use berlin.de only for Berlin's GOST Teil C files and Berlin-specific mirrors.

### Gaps
- Name and URL of Berlin's own Landesinstitut (successor to LISUM on the Berlin side) was not found in the fetched sources.
- The exact URL of the SH ministry (MBWFK) landing page was only seen as a link target (https://www.schleswig-holstein.de/DE/Landesregierung/III/iii_node.html), not fetched.

## Key question 2: Exact list of curriculum documents (Mathematik, Chemie, Physik, integrated science, Grundschule), status and transitions

### Takeaway
BE/BB: one Rahmenlehrplan 1-10 (Teil A, B, C; in force since 1 Aug 2017), with Teil C Mathematik revised 2023 (BB from 2023/24, BE from 2024/25, fully phased in 2024/25 resp. 2025/26); Chemie, Physik, Nawi 5/6, Nawi 7-10, Sachunterricht still the 2015 edition. GOST: ländergemeinsamer RLP since 2022/23 (Einführungsphase) and 2023/24 (Qualifikationsphase); Teil C Chemie and Physik newly written (2021/2022), Teil C Mathematik carried over. SH: Fachanforderungen Mathematik 2024 (Primar and Sek) phasing in since 2024/25; Chemie and Physik 2026 editions phase in from 2026/27, replacing the 2022 editions; Naturwissenschaften Sek I 2014 (amended 2018); Sachunterricht 2019. MV: a full new generation of Rahmenpläne is rolling out: Mathematik GS 2024 (from 2024/25), Orientierungsstufe 2026 (from 2026/27), Sek I Mathematik/Physik/Chemie and Sek II Chemie "2026" editions valid from 1 Aug 2027 aufwachsend; old plans run out by 31 Jul 2031.

### Inferences
- MV is mid-transition: for a learning assistant, the "old" Sek I plans remain authoritative for pupils already in grades 8-10 until 2031, while the 2026 editions govern cohorts entering grade 7 from 2027/28. Both sets must be kept, keyed by cohort.
- SH Chemie/Physik 2022 editions remain relevant for current Sek I cohorts; the 2026 editions start with the entry grade in 2026/27.
- The MV Sek II Mathematik and Physik plans show no dates on the page; the Physik file name says "Erprobungsfassung". Their status must be read from the PDF front matter.

## Key question 3: Legal basis, rights notices, § 5 UrhG assessment

### Takeaway
Brandenburg puts curricula into force by Verwaltungsvorschrift published in the Amtsblatt des MBJS (currently VVRLPBilst of 2 Oct 2025). SH puts Fachanforderungen into force by Erlass published in the Nachrichtenblatt (NBl. MBWK), and treats them as Verwaltungsvorschriften. For Berlin and MV the exact instrument was not located in the fetched pages. None of the four portals carries an open licence; IQSH and MV Impressa restrict reuse.

### Cited Findings
- BB: "Verwaltungsvorschrift über Rahmenlehrpläne, curriculare Materialien und Bildungsstandards an Schulen des Landes Brandenburg (VV-Rahmenlehrpläne-Bildungsstandards, VVRLPBilst)" of 2 October 2025, Abl. MBJS/25, Nr. 23, S. 354, amended 5 June 2026 (Abl. MBJS/26, Nr. 9, S. 90), effective 1 August 2025, legal basis § 10 Abs. 6 i.V.m. Abs. 3 and § 146 BbgSchulG; replaces the VV of 2019 and VV-Bildungsstandards 2005; Anlage 1 references KMK Bildungsstandards (e.g. MSA Biologie/Chemie/Physik 16.12.2004 i.d.F. 13.06.2024; AHR Bio/Che/Phy 18.06.2020; AHR Mathematik 18.10.2012), [VVRLPBilst](https://bravors.brandenburg.de/verwaltungsvorschriften/vvrlpbilst)
